07:02Sitrep

What moved while I was asleep?

SITREP is the pinned Research home. One backend request composes equities, FX, yields, commodities, events, prediction markets and news into a single triage picture, and each panel keeps its own freshness, provenance and failure state — one dead provider degrades one panel, not the screen.

Anything worth chasing gets starred as a follow-up, which persists on the backend, and hands off into the tab that actually owns the question.

07:19Macro

Is the policy read coherent, or is it fracturing?

Macro pulls raw FRED series, derives the YoY and spread series it needs, then scores how far the themed signals disagree. Divergence is strongest positive signal minus strongest negative; the coherence label asks when each supportive series reached roughly 60% of its current move, and names the leader and the laggard.

None of that is dressed up as a model. The labels read coherent, narrow or fractured, and the tab states plainly that they are heuristics.

07:36Commodities

Does the physical curve agree with the macro story?

Curves come from official EIA fundamentals, FRED spot proxies, or — with a connected TWS session and futures entitlements — read-only IBKR futures curves Gamma builds itself from discovered contract details.

Gamma labels contango, backwardation and flat; computes front spread, M1–M6, curve slope and a roll-yield proxy; and refuses to validate a futures row against a sample history. When a row has no daily reference, it says so instead of inventing one.

07:54Prediction Markets

What odds is anyone actually putting on it?

Polymarket and Kalshi normalised into one market record: a screener, probability history, wallet or taker-flow summaries, related-market consistency across venues, and a first-pass calibration view that compares pre-resolution probability against realised frequency on markets that already settled.

Research rank is Gamma's, not the venue's — a weighted mix of volume, liquidity, open interest and resolution timing, penalised for staleness. Freshness has five explicit states, including broken, for an "open" contract whose end time has passed.

08:11Fundamentals

Does the name survive its own filings?

Company analysis built directly on SEC EDGAR through EdgarTools: filing chronology, company facts, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow and ratios on annual or quarterly basis, with YoY and QoQ comparison and amendment context.

The DCF workbench holds Bear / Base / Bull scenarios, sensitivity, terminal-value framing and snapshots that persist locally — plus a reverse-valuation mode that asks what the current price already assumes.

08:29Options / IV

What is the option market charging for that move?

An IV surface snapshot over IBKR, with depth presets that trade expiry count against strike width inside a fixed TWS market-data-line budget. Expiry/strike heatmap, ATM term structure, front smile, wing skew, realized-vs-implied, and a local implied-probability density slice.

The strategy builder takes selected legs from the chain and returns net premium, breakevens, max profit and loss, and a payoff matrix across price and remaining days. Greeks are the provider's where IBKR supplies them and Gamma's Black-Scholes where it does not — the panel says which.

08:47Risk

What would this actually do to the book?

One engine, two workspaces: Portfolio mode analyses the live account snapshot, Research mode analyses a synthetic book handed over from Equity Research or Strategy Lab. Historical and parametric VaR, Monte Carlo under Gaussian draws or historical bootstrap, CVaR, rolling beta and correlation, and contribution-to-risk rankings.

When only part of the book has usable history, the headline is a scaled estimate and Gamma says so, with a coverage diagnostic naming every excluded asset.

09:05Copilot

Write it up — with the sources still attached.

Copilot is grounded in whatever Gamma has actually loaded. It adapts to the active tab and selected entity, keeps per-domain thread history, and exposes scope, provenance, warnings and tool traces inside the output rather than behind it.

Its authority control is visible and has two settings: a context-bounded Research Agent, or a Research Operator that can drive the app — and is still read-only. Bring your own OpenAI key, or run the mock provider offline.

The boundary is the product

Gamma is a research environment, not an execution platform. The read-only contract is exposed by the API itself at /system/read-only-boundary, covering both the TWS operator lock and the app-side refusal to execute.

What it does

  • Monitors a live or sample portfolio in your base currency
  • Builds single-name and synthetic research scopes
  • Forwards a scope from one tab into another, intact
  • Shows the arithmetic behind every derived value
  • Labels degraded, stale, partial and sample states explicitly
  • Runs entirely on your machine, on your own credentials
Gamma is designed to help you inspect data, compare signals, and understand the calculations behind the screens. It is not designed to place trades or to hide its analytics behind unexplained scores.
README.md

What it refuses

  • Placing orders or executing strategies
  • Acting as a trading bot or rebalancer
  • Giving personalised investment advice
  • Treating macro interpretation as a causal model
  • Presenting incomplete risk coverage as portfolio-wide truth
  • Running your code with network, credential or broker authority
