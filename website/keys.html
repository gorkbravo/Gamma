Every provider, and how to get in.

Gamma has no hosted backend and no shared credentials. It talks to providers as you, using keys you obtain yourself and paste into a local .env. Keys are read server-side; the frontend never receives one. Most of what Gamma uses is free, and the app runs end-to-end with none of them.

SEC EDGAR

Identity, not a key

Fundamentals reads company facts and filings from data.sec.gov through EdgarTools. The SEC requires every automated client to identify itself with a real name and email in the User-Agent — it is not authentication, it is their fair-access policy.

Cost
Free
Requires
Your name and email
Rate limit
10 requests / second

GAMMA_SEC_USER_NAME=… · GAMMA_SEC_USER_EMAIL=…

Set it to your own details. .env.example ships placeholders; fill them in before using Fundamentals. Whatever you put here is sent to data.sec.gov on every request.

Roadmap

How Gamma handles them

Four rules that hold across every provider.

Server-side only

Keys are read by the FastAPI process from your local .env. No key is compiled into the frontend or sent to the browser — AISstream is proxied through the backend precisely for this reason.

Never in an error

Provider exception text and credentials are not copied into user-facing warnings. A failed FRED call names the series, not your key.

Absence is a state

A missing key produces an explicit unavailable or degraded label. Gamma does not silently substitute sample data for a provider it could not reach.

Local, not shared

There is no Gamma account, no telemetry back to a vendor, and no server holding your credentials. The app runs on your machine.

.env 
# Copy .env.example to .env, then fill in what you have.
# Everything below is optional — Gamma runs with none of it.

FRED_API_KEY=your-fred-key
EIA_API_KEY=your-eia-key
CENSUS_API_KEY=your-census-key
AISSTREAM_API_KEY=your-aisstream-key
OPENAI_API_KEY=sk-your-openai-key

# Identify yourself to the SEC — use your own name and address.
GAMMA_SEC_USER_NAME=Your Name
GAMMA_SEC_USER_EMAIL=you@example.com

# Switch providers on once their keys are present.
COMMODITIES_PROVIDER=eia
MARITIME_PROVIDER=aisstream
